Good to know

Membership duration

Annual Membership includes 12 planned skincare sessions across a 12-month treatment schedule.

Appointment scheduling

Appointments are required and subject to availability.

Personalized treatments

Every facial is customized according to skin condition and suitability. Advanced peels or intensive procedures may not be appropriate at every visit.

Membership payments

Members choosing installment payments agree to the payment schedule stated in their signed agreement.

Unused sessions & cancellations

Unused session balances, expiration, rescheduling, cancellations and refunds are governed by the written membership agreement and applicable law.

VIP discounts

VIP discounts cannot be combined with other promotions unless otherwise specified.

Membership transfers

Memberships are intended for individual use. Any transfer is subject to written approval and applicable terms.
