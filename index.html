    Hi, I'm Vi — founder of V Angel Facial and Nail Spa.

    I built this business on my own, step by step. I have owned my nail salon for 9 years, and 2 years ago I opened our facial and spa side — because I wanted to take care of the whole beauty of a woman, not just her nails.

    Everything I do, I do with my heart. I choose products carefully, I work slowly and honestly, and I always aim for the best possible result for your skin — healthy, glowing, and safe.

    That is V Angel: real skill, real care, and real results.
