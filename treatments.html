PMU & Brows

Brows mapped to your face

Every brow starts with measuring your features, so the shape fits you and nobody else. Smudge-proof, wake-up-ready brows that last.

Please wait to book if you

  • Are pregnant or breastfeeding
  • Took Accutane in the past year
  • Are going through chemotherapy
  • Have a sunburn, rash or irritation on your brow area

Message us first if you

  • Have diabetes, a bleeding or clotting disorder, or keloid scarring
  • Take blood thinners
  • Had Botox or filler on your forehead in the last 2 weeks

Before your appointment

  • No alcohol or caffeine for 24 hours
  • No aspirin, ibuprofen or fish oil for 48 hours, unless your doctor prescribed them. Never stop prescribed medicine without asking your doctor.
  • No brow waxing, threading or tinting for 1 week
  • No retinol or face acids near your brows for 2 weeks
  • No tanning for 2 weeks
